Factors To Consider When You Build the Solution

1. Use Case Understanding and Relevance - clarity and depth in terms of understanding the problem statement

2. Solution Architecture - quality and feasibility of the overall architecture design, how well do the components interact and integrate with each other

3. Innovation and Creativity - level of innovation and creativity in the solution, originality of ideas, innovative use of AI/ML.

4. UI and UX - usability and design of the UI, ease of navigation, visual appeal etc.

5. Technical Implementation and Source Code Quality - code readability, use of best practices, documentation etc.

6. Model Performance and Evaluation - model accuracy/precision/recall, F1 score and other relevant metrics

7. Deployment and Integration - use of CI/CD pipelines, cloud deployment strategies, integration with APIs etc.

8. Presentation and Communication - clarity of communication, organization of the presentation, ability to answer questions and justify design choices

9. Collaboration and Teamwork - Team dynamics, distribution of tasks and overall collaboration
